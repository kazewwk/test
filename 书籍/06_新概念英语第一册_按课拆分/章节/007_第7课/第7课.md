## Notes on the text 课文注释

1 My name's = My name is.

2 I'm /aɪm/ = I am.

口语中经常使用这种缩略形式。

3 What's your job? 你是做什么工作的?

What's = What is.

4 What nationality are you?

用来询问对方国籍。也可以问 Where are you from?
