## move to sw.

do/does: He always moves to Hainan every winter. did: He moved to Hainan last winter.

have done: He has just moved to Hainan. am/is/are doing: He is moving to Hainan next month.

## knock at the door

Someone is knocking at the door. Is someone knocking at the door? Who is knocking at the door?
