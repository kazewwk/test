## have something done...

含义: cause something to be done by somebody else.

I must have my watch repaired.

I must get my watch repaired.

I'm going to have my hair cut this afternoon.

I'm going to get my hair cut this afternoon.

## 时态:

I had my hair cut yesterday.

We are having the house painted this week.

I'm going to have my hair cut this afternoon.

He has his hair cut every month.

## did vs have something done

Lisa repaired the roof.

Lisa had the roof repaired.

I made those curtains.

I had those curtains made.

## have something done

意外，不幸的遭遇。翻译成被。

Gary had his nose broken in a fight.

I had my bag stolen.

Have you ever had your bike stolen?
