# have+done 用法一名称：现在完成时 NCE1L83-L88含义：过去的事情对现在产生了影响(言之过去，意在当下)

## 结构

肯定句 have/has+done A: I can help to clean his shoes. B: He has cleaned his shoes. (His shoes are clean now)

A: Do you want to have dinner with us? B: I have just had some food. (I'm full now.)

A: Are they at home? B: They have gone out. (They are not at home now.)

否定句 have/has+not+done I haven't had breakfast.

一般疑问句have/has提前&done不变 Have you had breakfast?

## have+done 用法一

have+done=did+do/does A: I can help to clean his shoes. B: He has cleaned his shoes. (His shoes are clean now) He cleaned his shoes, and now his shoes are clean.

## have+done=did+do/does

A: Do you want to have dinner with us? B: I have just had some food. (I'm full now.) I had some food, and now I'm full.

have+done=did+do/does   
A: Are they at home?   
B: They have gone out.   
(They are not at home now.)   
They went out, and now they are not at home.

# have+done 用法二名称：现在完成时 NCE1L89-L90含义：从过去持续到现在的事情

结构：

肯定句 have/has+done

I have lived in Beijing for 10 years/since 2012.

I have lived in Beijing for 10 years.

I lived in Beijing for 10 years.

否定句have/has+not+done

I haven't eaten anything since breakfast.

(from breakfast until now)

一般疑问句have/has提前&done不变

Have you heard anything from Berlin since yesterday?
