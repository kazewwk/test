## enter for

[Story]: Every year I enter for the   
garden competition.   
Do you enter for the garden competition every year?

## make a path

He made a path in his garden.   
He is making a path in his garden.   
He has made a path in his garden.   
He was making a path in his garden.   
He is going to make a path in his garden.

## build a bridge

They built a bridge over the river last year. Did they build a bridge over the river last year?   
why/who/when/where

## win a prize

He won a big prize last month. Did he win a big prize last month? How did he win a big prize last month?

## I∫aυtl shout v.

1 shout at sb. (This suggests that we're angry.) 2 shout to sb. (When we shout to someone, we want them to hear us. They might be on the other side of the street)
