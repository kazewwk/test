would do VS used to do

L31: used to do sth. would 过去重复发生的动作

异1: 过去状态(past state)只用 used to do

1. This sort of novel used to be very popular.

2. I used to have long hair.

3. He used to be the head of a very large business company.

## 异2: used to do 暗含现在不发生了

1. He used to/would bring me little presents.

## 异3: 用used to do 而非 would 谈论[经常]且[重要]的[习

惯行为]。— Michael Swan“Practical English Usage

1. Robert used to play a lot of football.

2. I used to smoke.

3. I would smoke. ???

注意:

1. 并不很重要

2. 大量输入提升语感

3. 语法书和人们使用习惯的区别
