## 过去完成进行时

肯定句：主+had been doing..

否定句:主+had not been doing..

一般疑问句: Had 主 been doing...?

用法:现在完成进行时的过去时

## 用法一:过去A进行的v.(结束)对过去B有影响。

•Yesterday morning I got up and looked out of the window. The sun was shining but the ground was very wet.

•It had been raining.

She had been crying. Her eyes were red. She had been running. She was out of breath.

I had been working all day.

When I got home, I was very tired.
