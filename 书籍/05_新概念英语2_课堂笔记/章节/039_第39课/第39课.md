## 虚拟语气

## 结构:

If +did/were,

主 + would/should/could/might do.

did 并非表示过去

If I were you, I wouldn't do that.

If I knew them better, I might invite them to dinner.

## 含义：

1. if从句中v.与现在事实相反

2. 说话人认为，if从句中v.发生的几率非常低

If I were you, I wouldn't do that.

If I knew them better, I might invite them to dinner.

It's cold. If I were you, I would put my coat on.
