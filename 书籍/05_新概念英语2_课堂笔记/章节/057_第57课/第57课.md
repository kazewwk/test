## My secretary sent the bill to Mr. Harding yesterday.

1. It was my secretary that sent the bill to Mr. Harding yesterday.

2. It was the bill that my secretary sent to Mr. Harding yesterday.

3. It was Mr. Harding that my secretary sent the bill to yesterday.

4. It was yesterday that my secretary sent the bill to Mr. Harding.
