## References

A concise list of selected references is included at the end of each chapter. These are arranged in alphabetical order by author surname under the main chapter section headings. These references often include the original papers in which the most critical discoveries were first reported. The ebook also includes the DOI identifier for the references, making it easy for students to access the articles.

## Glossary Terms

Throughout the book, boldface type has been used to highlight key terms at the point in a chapter where the main discussion occurs. Italic type is used to set off important terms with a lesser degree of emphasis. At the end of the book is an expanded glossary, covering all the major terms common to cell biology; it should be the first resort for a reader who encounters an unfamiliar technical word.
